# Persistent navigation backdrop

The wallpaper and perspective floor belong to the application viewport, outside both cube renderers. Navigation keeps the same floor DOM and CSS drift instance. The floor does not inherit the cube’s zoom blur, scale, or foreground opacity, and the page dialog no longer paints a second opaque full-screen background or replacement wallpaper over it.

Wallpaper and ground drift keep running during page opening, scrolling, and closing. They pause only while the browser tab is hidden or the initial assets are not ready; reduced-motion preferences still disable drift. Pointer/touch parallax pauses during navigation so gestures inside the page retain normal scrolling without moving the ground plane. The decorative light pool fades during approach, while the cube’s ground shadow remains painted and follows its rendered pose.

Homepage branding and chrome remain mounted, visible, opaque, and stationary behind the cube. Opening a face now advances the cube along its own world Z axis while holding all three camera coordinates fixed. The ground shadow uses the same cube position, camera position, and vertical FOV in both renderers. Its footprint moves and scales as the cube approaches, naturally leaving the viewport when it passes below the camera view; closing reverses the projection. Shadow updates use one compositor transform, with no independent tween, CSS lag, or extra render loop. Closing returns that same cube from its held position to Z=0. Responsive updates refit the cube's depth, including while the renderer sleeps, without moving the camera or scaling the homepage.

During the dissolve, a shared projected cube silhouette preserves the solid cube's occlusion of the homepage UI as the canvas fades. The UI does not move, fade out, or unmount. Background grids use separate layers, so they remain visible and animate outside the page panel. The silhouette updates from the actual worker/fallback cube pose and clears after the page closes.
The zoomed cube face stays painted during page entrance and crossfades its opacity with the page, then becomes hidden only once the page is fully open. Closing reverses that crossfade before returning the camera. Page readiness is committed in a layout effect after establishing the initial zero-opacity style. Camera/blur timing and reduced-motion behavior are retained.

Browser regressions sample the same sharp, fully opaque floor across navigation, verify actual floor pixels below the panel, and check that both tiled animations advance while the page is open. Page gestures do not move the ground plane. Cases include all four pages, both themes, touch, fallback rendering, and reduced motion. Cube transition checks verify simultaneous fractional cube/page opacity, stationary and fully rendered homepage UI, constant camera coordinates while cube depth increases, responsive refitting, and normal or interrupted closing.

The HTML loader and live floor share the same tiled pseudo-element, origin, cell spacing, and paused phase. Neither floor interpolates its perspective transform during startup. Drift resumes through the loader fade; pointer parallax transitions become available only after readiness. Repeated reload checks compare the paused loader/live tiles and sample the floor transform frame by frame.
